Who Is This Person?

Lei Singha is a Master of Digital Media student at Toronto Metropolitan University, a computer science graduate, and a part-time menace in the boxing ring. By day he designs sound for a VR meditation experience. By night he punches things at the gym. Nobody has asked how these two hobbies fit together. He would prefer you didn't.

Why should the internet care? Because he has written code in Java, Python, TypeScript and now HTML, and every single one of those languages has crashed on him at least once. He is still standing.

Origin Story

Our hero's family comes from Sylhet, Bangladesh, a region famous for tea gardens, rain, and very strong opinions about food. He crossed an ocean to come to Canada as a student, armed with nothing but a laptop and an unreasonable belief that winter "can't be that bad."

Winter was that bad. Undeterred, he enrolled in Computer Science at TMU, survived co-op terms at the Ontario Public Service and a pension plan, and learned that most problems in life can be solved with either a Power Automate flow or a left hook. He then decided one degree was not enough suffering and started a master's.
