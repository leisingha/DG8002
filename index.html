Who Is This Person?

Lei Singha is a Master of Digital Media student at Toronto Metropolitan University, a computer science graduate, and a man with an almost supernatural ability to turn a two-hour task into a two-week one. By day he designs sound for a VR meditation experience. By night he is three movies deep into a watchlist that was supposed to be "just one episode."

Why should the internet care? Because he has written code in Java, Python, TypeScript and now HTML, finished every assignment at the last possible minute, and somehow still has strong opinions about which Toronto restaurant does the best all-you-can-eat deal.

Origin Story

Our hero's family comes from Sylhet, Bangladesh, a region famous for tea gardens, rain, and very strong opinions about food. He crossed an ocean to come to Canada as a student, armed with nothing but a laptop and an unreasonable belief that winter "can't be that bad."

Winter was that bad. Undeterred, he enrolled in Computer Science at TMU, survived co-op terms at the Ontario Public Service and a pension plan, and discovered that most deadlines can be met with either a Power Automate flow or a caffeine-fuelled all-nighter the night before. He then decided one degree was not enough suffering and started a master's.
